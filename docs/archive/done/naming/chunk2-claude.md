# Special recipes, Chunk 2 (Claude)

Ten special recipes, two for each of Telluric/Aqueous, Telluric/Voltaic, Pyric/Aqueous, Pyric/Voltaic and Aqueous/Voltaic. A recipe is an order-independent pair of two specific base species (one from each type) that produces a unique hybrid instead of the type pair's default hybrid. Column layout follows the Default hybrids table in `content-data.md`, plus Parent species, Animal, Cosmetic description and Aether-Log hint.

Conventions used (all taken from the existing default hybrids):
- Covered skills are the type-level skills of both parent types, primary skill first. The primary skill is always one of the two parents' own primary skills.
- Every ability carries a damage type (also on heals, shields and buffs), and it is one of the two parent types.
- Every signature trait is written "Name: mechanic while working <primary skill> [strength]", so it only triggers on the primary skill.

## Self-review: what I could not fully satisfy or verify

1. **Rule 1 (forms are the same animal) is not machine-checkable.** I chose every suffix by hand for the stated animal. The weakest spots: Flintlamb is a ram whose Form 1 is the lamb of the same animal, so a strict reader may object; Coalgrub's Form 1 is the larval stage of the firefly (still a creature with a face and body, but a grub); Pulsedart and Sprayfledge name a small fast bird less explicitly than the other forms do; "Brandtail" leans on the tail alone to say fox.
2. **Rule 2, "must not duplicate".** I read it as: no trait shares a mechanic with either of its two parents' traits, and no two special hybrids share a mechanic (10 traits, 10 mechanics; verified). Some traits do repeat the mechanic of a species that is not their parent (Shell Hoard with Splashfin, Careful Skimmer with Roastbelly, Beacon Lure with Plasmaplug), which design.md's shared global cap already covers. Charged Wake is the mirror of the default hybrid Brinecore (Aqueous drop while on Circuitry, against a Voltaic drop while on Fishing); different, but the same pattern.
3. **Stat-bonus traits are a judgment call.** Iron Skull (Guard), Unbothered (Health) and Sly Temper (Power) use "bonus Health/Power/Guard" from section 4, gated to the primary skill as required. A stat bonus that only applies while the creature is on a skill task is allowed by the letter of section 4, but its practical value depends on how the sim treats stats during work. Swap these for extra-output or rare-drop traits if that reads as pointless. Nothing else in the batch depends on them.
4. **Palette reading.** Each hybrid and form name starts with one root from a palette of one of its two parent types (checked), and every recipe draws on both palettes across its three forms (checked). The second half of each name is an animal-part word (nip, claw, snout, plume, and so on), not a second palette root as in the Chunk 1 renames (Sorrelcliff and so on), so that each form visibly fits its animal.
5. **Uniqueness against Chunk 1.** The corrected Chunk 1 result is not in the repo, so I could only check against the 10 locked renames, not against Chunk 1's forms, abilities or traits. Palette roots overlap those renames in different positions (Fluxwing and Ivyflux, Boltkit and Burrbolt, Mesahorn and Nettlemesa, and so on); the full names are all different.
6. **Suffix counting.** "Suffix" means the part after the palette root. The only repeat is "-eel" (Kelpeel, Dynamoeel), which is exactly at the limit of two.
7. **Not machine-checkable, judged by me:** no real-world IP (all names are invented compounds or plain English words), hints being genuinely riddle-like and not too easy, and the descriptions being cosmetic (checked only for mechanic words).
