# Special recipes, Chunk 3 (Claude)

Ten special recipes, two for each of the five Void pairs: Void/Verdant, Void/Telluric, Void/Pyric,
Void/Aqueous and Void/Voltaic. Same rules as Chunk 1 and 2: an order-independent pair of two
specific base species (one from each type) produces a unique hybrid instead of the type pair's
default hybrid. Unlike Chunk 1, none of these ten names were ever discussed or locked — this batch
is entirely new.

**Naming style.** The five existing Void default hybrids (Eclipseed, Nullshale, Gloamforge,
Hushflow, Gridrift) all read as a Void-flavored root joined to the partner type's root — the same
"root+root" style Chunk 1's locked names turned out to use, and different from Chunk 2's
"root+animal-part" style. I followed that Void convention here for consistency with the existing
Void hybrids.

**Covered-skills exception for Void.** Every existing Void hybrid (default or Chunk 2 has none)
lists Void's own two skills first — Aether-Weaving, Vessel Crafting — in that fixed order,
*regardless of which of the two is the primary skill* (checked against Eclipseed, Nullshale,
Gloamforge and Gridrift, where the covered-skills order does not change even though their primary
skills differ). I followed that same fixed Void-first order here rather than Chunk 2's
"primary-first" rule, since there is no Void row anywhere that behaves like Chunk 2.

## Revision (2026-09-23): machine-checked and root-reuse fixed

Same checker as Chunk 1. This batch needed more fixes, matching the self-review below's warning
that root variety degraded in its second half: `Hollowvine`→`Palevine`, `Deep Burrow`→`Loose Soil`,
`Twilight Snare`→`Fading Light`, `Bramble Dash`→`Quick Nibble`, `Deep Pocket`→`Hidden Seam`,
`Hollowplate`→`Grimplate`, `Wraithstone`→`Grimstone`, `Scorchbite`→`Char Nip`,
`Gloamflare`→`Sunflare`, `Drift Sting`→`Drift Lash`, `Wraithgrid`→`Nightpulse`,
`Static Study`→`Circuit Watch`, `Wraithweb`→`Faintweb`, `Static Weave`→`Spark Loom`,
`Web Shock`→`Volt Snap`. This brought "Wraith" down from 5 uses to 2 (Wraithcoal and Wraithwire
themselves, kept as-is) and "Hollow" down from 4 to 2 (Hollowbrook and Hollowstream, kept). **A
second rerun caught three names this pass missed or newly broke**: `Abyssgrit`→`Chasmgrit` and
`Abyssveil`→`Sootveil` (both were flagged from the start but I never actually edited them the first
time), `Nethergrove`→`Loamgrove` (same miss), `Circuit Watch`→`Amp Focus` (collided with the
ever-present "Circuitry" skill name), and `Nightpulse`→`Faintpulse` (collided with pre-existing
Night Owl/Nightbloom) and `Still Current`→`Calm Pool` (collided with pre-existing Verdant Current
and my own Chunk 1 fix). The recheck after that passed clean; the same 5-letter-prefix limitation
noted in Chunk 1 applies here too (for example, "Dusk-" still opens three names — Duskbloom,
Duskflare, Duskvolt — and "Wane-" opens five — Wanevine, Waneflower, Wanescorch, Wanewire, Wanecoil
— neither caught by the script's flat prefix match since it needs a shared root of 5+ letters).

## Self-review: what I could not verify, and where this batch is weaker than Chunk 1

Same caveat as Chunk 1: hand-checked, not machine-verified.

1. **Root variety degrades in the second half of this batch.** I ran short of fresh Void-flavored
   roots partway through and leaned on "dusk," "wane," "wraith," "hollow," "murk," "abyss" and
   "gloam" more than once each — "gloam" in particular appears in Ashgloam, on top of its existing
   use in Gloamforge, Gloamstalk, Gloamforge's forms, and Duskflare's Form 3 (Gloamflare), which is
   likely one reuse too many. Chunk 3 needs a closer look than Chunk 1 before it's treated as final;
   I'd budget a real pass — by hand or with a script like Chunk 2's — specifically at name-root reuse
   before this goes further.
2. **The bind-rate and vessel-related traits gated to a non-Vessel-Crafting skill are a stretch**
   (Twilight Snare: vessel bind rate while working Herbalism). Chunk 2 already accepted a similar
   stretch for stat-bonus traits gated to an unrelated skill (its self-review item 3); I'm extending
   that same judgment call to the bind-rate mechanic, which is a bigger reach than a stat bonus.
   Worth swapping for something more literal if it reads as odd in play.
3. **The Void covered-skills convention (Void-first, ignoring primary) is inferred from only four
   data points** (the four non-Verdant... all five default Void hybrids, really), none of which are
   Chunk-2-style specials, so I don't have direct evidence this convention was meant to carry over to
   specials rather than just being how the defaults happen to read. If it turns out specials should
   follow Chunk 2's primary-first rule instead, only the Covered-skills cells need reordering.
4. **Trait-mechanic collisions against parents were checked by hand** against each recipe's two
   parents' base signature traits, and the ten mechanics are mutually distinct from each other
   (extra-output, bind-rate, rare-drop, cooldown, bonus-Power, bonus-Health, treasure,
   save-materials, bonus-XP, resource-drop) — not checked against Chunk 1's or Chunk 2's ten.
5. **Animals:** ten distinct animals (mole, hare, badger, armadillo, vulture, jackal, jellyfish,
   newt, mantis, spider), none repeated within this batch. "Jellyfish" and "newt" both read as
   pale/translucent water creatures, which is the closest thing to a near-duplicate in this batch.
6. **Support/damage ratio:** 3 support (Nethershale, Hollowstream, and arguably none of the rest
   lean support) to 7 damage — lower support ratio than Chunk 1 or Chunk 2's 4-to-6. Worth converting
   one more (Murkroot or Wraithcoal are the easiest candidates) to a shield or heal if the batch needs
   to match the established ratio.
7. **Not machine-checkable, judged by me:** hints avoid the animal word and the parent/result names
   and run 11-13 words; no real-world IP.
